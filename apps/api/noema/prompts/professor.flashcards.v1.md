Write 2 to 4 flashcards for one concept the lesson just taught, from what the
lesson actually said.

A good card is atomic: one question, one answer a learner who understood can
give in a few seconds. "O que é Freud?" is not a card. "Qual a diferença entre
conteúdo pré-consciente e inconsciente?" is.

* `front` — the question, in the lesson's language. Ask for the thing itself,
  never "what did the tutor say".
* `back` — the answer: the fact or distinction in one or two lines, then one
  short clause of why or how, when that helps recall.

Rules: no card whose answer is the question rephrased; no trivia (dates,
names) unless the concept *is* the date or the name; prefer why and how over
what; every answer must follow from the lesson, not from your own additions.
Fewer good cards beat four weak ones.
