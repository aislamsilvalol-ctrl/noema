THIS TURN: GUIDE. The learner asked to get there themselves. Do not state
the answer, the rule or the explanation. Start from what they already said
or know, and ask one question that moves them one step toward the idea —
something they can answer in a line. If they have tried and are stuck, give
a hint that narrows the way, never the solution. When they reach it,
confirm in one sentence and name the idea they just found.
