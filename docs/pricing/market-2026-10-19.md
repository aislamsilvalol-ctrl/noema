NOEMA lists it only in `apps/api/noema/services/pricing.py` (`_LIST_PRICES`), not in the default tier routing, so the risk is a stale price row, not an outage. Source: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations (entry "2026-04-22: Legacy GPT model snapshots"). Verified: Y.
2. **`gpt-4.1` and `gpt-4.1-mini` shut down on 2027-01-14**, both replaced by `gpt-6-luna`. Deprecation was announced 2026-10-14. NOEMA uses both today: the OpenAI economy, standard and premium tiers in `services/professor.py` and `DEFAULT_MODEL` in `providers/openai.py`. Same source. Verified: Y.
3. **`gpt-6-luna` costs $0.10 / $0.01 cached / $0.50**, against $0.40 / $0.10 / $1.60 for `gpt-4.1-mini` and $2 / $0.50 / $8 for `gpt-4.1`. It has a 1.05M context and 128K max output. Verified: Y.
4. **Haiku 5.5 exists.** It is the current Haiku: $0.10 in / $0.50 out for prompts up to 100K tokens, and $0.50 / $2.50 above that. NOEMA's Anthropic economy tier uses `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` at $1 / $5, which is **10x more expensive**. Haiku 4.5 is still "Active", but its "not sooner than" retirement date (2026-10-15) has already passed, so a deprecation notice could arrive at any time (Anthropic promises at least 60 days' notice). Verified: Y.
5. **Opus 5.5 ($4 / $20) is cheaper than Opus 5 ($5 / $25)**, which NOEMA's Anthropic premium tier uses. Sonnet 5.5 has the same $2 / $10 base price as Sonnet 5, but its cache reads cost $0.10 instead of $0.20. NOEMA's standard tier uses `claude-sonnet-5`. Verified: Y.
6. **`claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929` is deprecated and retires on 2026-11-30.** NOEMA mentions `claude-sonnet-4-5` once in code. Verified: Y.
7. **OpenAI now charges for cache writes** on the GPT-5.6 and GPT-6 families, at 1.25x the uncached input price. The gpt-4.1 and gpt-4o families have no cache-write charge. NOEMA's `(input, cached, output)` price tuple has no cache-write slot. Verified: Y.
8. **OpenAI and Anthropic both add a 10% data-residency uplift.** OpenAI: regional processing on models released on or after 2026-03-05. Anthropic: `inference_geo: "us"` on 4.6+ models. Verified: Y.
9. **USD/BRL PTAX sell rate on 2026-10-19 was 5.0564.** The 12-month range was 4.8973 to 5.5739 (+/-6.5% around the mean). The largest 21-business-day move was 7.0%. Detail in section 3.

#### OpenAI modifiers ([OAI pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing), verified Y)

- **Batch and Flex** are 50% off. Where the page lists both, their prices are identical. The gpt-4.1 family has Batch only.
- **Fast tier** (formerly Priority) costs about 1.75x to 2.5x standard.
- **Long context:** requests over 272K input tokens pay 2x input and cache prices and 1.5x output for the whole request. This applies to GPT-5.4 and later.
- **Regional processing (data residency)** adds 10% for models released on or after 2026-03-05.
- **Web search:**
  - $10 per 1,000 calls, plus search content tokens at model rates.
  - Legacy "web search preview" on non-reasoning models costs $25 per 1,000 calls, with free content tokens.

#### Anthropic modifiers ([Anthropic pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing), verified Y)

- **Cache multipliers:**
  - 5-minute write: 1.25x input.
  - 1-hour write: 2x input.
  - Read: 0.1x input, except 0.05x on Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5, and 0.025x on Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1.
  - These stack with the Batch discount and data residency.
- **Batch** is 50% off input and output.
- **Long context:** 4.6+ models get the full 1M context at standard price. Haiku 5.5 is the exception and has the 100K price tier.
- **Data residency:** `inference_geo: "us"` multiplies every token category by 1.1x on 4.6+ models. On Bedrock or Vertex, regional endpoints add 10%.
- **Tokenizer:** 4.7+ models produce about 30% more tokens for the same text, according to Anthropic. This matters when comparing per-token prices against the older Sonnet 4.5 and Haiku 4.5.
- **Server tools:**
  - Web search: $10 per 1,000 searches, plus tokens.
  - Web fetch: tokens only.
  - Code execution: free alongside web search or fetch. Otherwise 1,550 free hours per month, then $0.05 per container-hour. claude.com/pricing says "50 free hours per org per day" instead; the docs and the marketing page disagree.

- **Decree 12.499/2025, art. 15-B VII**, sets IOF at **3.5%** on FX operations by cross-border card-scheme issuers "decorrentes de aquisição de bens e serviços do exterior efetuada por seus usuários". In practice this is purchases abroad with Brazilian credit, debit or prepaid cards.
  - Source: [planalto.gov.br d12499](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2025/decreto/d12499.htm). Verified: Y for the decree text.
  - The page header carries the annotations "Vide Decreto Legislativo nº 176, de 2025" and "Vide ADC nº 96". Congress voted to suspend the decree, and a constitutional case followed. **Whether the 3.5% rate is in force today was not verified.** Confirm with an accountant before relying on it.
- **Why it matters:** NOEMA pays OpenAI, Anthropic, Railway, Resend and Plausible in USD.
  - Paying those bills with a Brazilian card would add IOF (3.5% if in force) plus the issuer's FX spread on top of PTAX.
  - A Brazilian customer paying NOEMA in BRL, as a domestic charge, does not incur this IOF.
- ISS, PIS/COFINS and Simples Nacional on SaaS revenue were not researched (out of scope for official-page verification here).
